How it works

Our pool removal methods

We take care of the entire process — city permits, inspections, and the removal method that fits your property and budget. We'll walk you through which method fits your project and your wallet during your free quote, no surprises.

Partial demolition / cave-in removal

We break down the top 18–36 inches of the pool shell, punch drainage holes in the base, and prepare the cavity for stable backfill — the most cost-effective option for most yards.

Complete pool demolition

We fully break up and extract the entire concrete pool shell, leaving nothing buried in your yard — ideal for future construction or stricter city codes.

Debris hauling & cleanup

All concrete, rebar, and debris is loaded up and hauled off your property with our own fleet — no mess left behind, no dumpster sitting in your driveway.

Expert compaction & grading

The step most crews rush. We backfill in careful lifts and compact as we go, so you're left with a graded, usable yard instead of a spot that sinks in a year.
