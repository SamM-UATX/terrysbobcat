Removal method

Partial removal

The middle ground between cave-in and complete removal. More of the shell comes out than a basic cave-in — often because your city's permit rules call for it — while still burying the rest in place to keep costs down.

  1. 1
    Assessment & permit

    We check what your specific city requires — some jurisdictions mandate more material removed than a simple cave-in allows — and pull the correct permit.

  2. 2
    Break up the shell

    A larger portion of the pool structure is broken up than with a cave-in — walls and floor sections — while the deepest material stays in place.

  3. 3
    Clear drainage & debris

    Drainage holes are opened through remaining concrete, and loose rubble is broken down small enough to bury safely.

  4. 4
    Backfill in compacted lifts

    Soil goes in layer by layer, compacted at each stage, so the extra material removed doesn't leave voids that settle later.

  5. 5
    Grade & inspect

    Final grading and city sign-off, same as every method — you're left with a yard that's actually ready to use.

Good fit if…

Your city's permit requirements go beyond a basic cave-in, or you want more of the old structure gone without paying for full extraction and hauling.
