Removal method

The cave-in method

The most budget-friendly way to remove a pool, and the right fit for most yards. We break down the top of the shell, open up the base for drainage, and bury the rest in place — instead of hauling every last piece of concrete off your property.

  1. 1
    Free assessment & permit

    We look at your specific pool and yard, confirm cave-in is allowed by your city, and pull the permit before any equipment shows up.

  2. 2
    Break the top of the shell

    We break down the top 18–36 inches of the pool — the bond beam and coping — using a compact excavator that can get into tight side yards.

  3. 3
    Open the base for drainage

    Large holes are punched through the bottom of the shell so water can't collect and pool underground after backfill.

  4. 4
    Backfill in compacted lifts

    The cavity is filled with soil in careful layers, compacting as we go — not dumped in all at once — so the yard doesn't sink later.

  5. 5
    Grade & inspect

    We grade the finished surface and get it signed off by the city inspector, so you're left with a clean, usable yard.

Good fit if…

You want the most cost-effective option, your city allows cave-in removal, and you're not planning heavy construction directly over the old pool footprint.
