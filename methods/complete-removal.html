Removal method

Complete removal

The full job: the entire concrete pool shell is broken up and extracted, and every bit of debris leaves your property. Nothing old stays buried — the cleanest slate if you're planning to build over the space later.

  1. 1
    Assessment & permit

    We confirm complete removal is what your project actually needs — it costs more than cave-in or partial, so we make sure it's worth it for your plans before pulling the permit.

  2. 2
    Break up the entire shell

    Walls, floor, bond beam — the whole structure is broken into haulable pieces using excavators sized to your access.

  3. 3
    Haul every load off-site

    Concrete and rebar are loaded straight into our own dump trucks and trailers — no dumpster sitting in your driveway, nothing left buried in your yard.

  4. 4
    Backfill with fresh material

    Since nothing old remains, the cavity is filled from scratch with clean soil in compacted lifts — a stable base for whatever comes next.

  5. 5
    Grade & inspect

    Final compaction, grading, and city inspection sign-off — ready to build on or landscape immediately.

Good fit if…

You're planning to build a structure (like an ADU) directly over the old pool footprint, or your city code requires full extraction rather than cave-in or partial removal.
